Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our platform

Is there a free trial?

Yes, our Professional plan comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required to start. The Starter plan is free forever for up to 10 employees.

Can I upgrade or downgrade anytime?

Absolutely. You can change your plan at any time from your billing settings. Changes take effect properly at the start of the next billing cycle.

Is my data secure?

Security is our top priority. We use bank-level encryption (AES-256) for all data at rest and in transit. We perform regular security audits and backups.

Do you offer custom integrations?

Yes, our Enterprise plan includes custom integrations with your existing HR and payroll systems. Contact our sales team for details.
